About me

Curiosity, structure and a bias for learning by building.

I combine economics, statistics and computer science to understand not only how a model works, but whether the data beneath it can be trusted.

01 / MY PATH

Economics→Data→Systems

I found my place where data, technology and real-world questions meet.

My academic path began with Economics and Big Data, where I learned to connect quantitative methods with the decisions organisations make. Today, in the MSc in Data Science at Sapienza, I am deepening the technical side of that connection.

I am especially interested in the full data lifecycle: relational design, ingestion, cleaning, efficient querying, modelling and clear communication. Studying abroad in Lithuania and Belgium also taught me to adapt quickly, work across cultures and approach unfamiliar problems with confidence.
