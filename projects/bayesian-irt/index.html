Case study

Bayesian Modelling

Bayesian Item Response Theory

Analysed Grade 10 INVALSI Italian-test data with Bayesian Rasch/1PL and 2PL models, comparing fit and ability estimates.

01 / RESEARCH QUESTION

Research question

How do Bayesian Rasch/1PL and 2PL models compare when applied to Grade 10 INVALSI Italian-test data?

02 / DATA

Data

The analysis used Grade 10 INVALSI Italian-test data from approximately 3,500 students across 49 items.

03 / TECHNICAL APPROACH

Technical approach

Bayesian Rasch/1PL and 2PL Item Response Theory models were implemented in R with JAGS and rjags.

04 / MAIN RESULTS

Main results

The 2PL model achieved better fit according to log loss, AIC, BIC and DIC. Ability estimates closely matched official WLE scores, with an approximate correlation of 0.990.

05 / TECHNOLOGIES

Technologies

  • R
  • JAGS
  • rjags
  • Bayesian statistics
  • Item Response Theory
